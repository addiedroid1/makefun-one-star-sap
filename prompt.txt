Build a make.fun entry for the bounty "One Star, Written in Sap": write a
program (main.ts) that writes a single one-line 1-star review from a
houseplant about its human owner to /output/entry.txt. The line must be
1 to 279 characters, one line, a genuine review with the plant's own
grievance at the centre, funny through a sharp plant's-eye complaint,
with fresh specific detail and no stock phrases about drama queens,
dramatic wilting or being ignored. Draft at least 6 genuinely different
candidate lines, verify every character count, blind-pick the best, and
ship only the picked line.
